# Pricing

## Options

- **Monthly plan:** 29 per month per studio, unlimited bookings.
- **Per booking:** 1% of each payment, no monthly fee.

## What we heard

Beta studios with fewer than 40 bookings a month prefer per booking. Busier studios prefer a flat fee. [[Maya Chen]] leans towards offering both.
